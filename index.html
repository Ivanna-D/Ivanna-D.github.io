Ivanna Dovbysh

Іванна Довбиш · Robotics & Computer Vision

I am a Fulbright scholar and M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Central Florida, where I work in the Learning, Communications, and Wireless Networks Lab with Prof. Nazanin Rahnavard.

I am currently a visiting researcher at the AirLab at Carnegie Mellon University with Prof. Sebastian Scherer, supported by an Edmund S. Muskie Professional Fellowship. There I build people-detection systems for the DARPA Triage Challenge, including thermal–NIR fusion for detecting people at night.

My research focuses on helping robots and assistive systems perceive the world reliably: detecting people in difficult conditions, estimating state from fused sensors, and guiding agents safely around moving obstacles. I received my B.S. with Honors in Automation and Instrumentation from Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute in 2025, where I worked on quadrotor UAV attitude estimation.

🏆 My video “Standards for the Usefulness of AI in Robotics” received the IROS 2026 Video Award!

Research Interests

Robust Perception for Robots

Detecting people and objects in hard, real-world conditions such as darkness, shadows, reflections and clutter, using data curation, model benchmarking and multimodal (thermal, NIR, RGB) sensor fusion.

State Estimation & Sensor Fusion for UAVs

Attitude estimation, inertial navigation and SLAM that combine IMU, optical flow, cameras and LiDAR, plus using onboard sensor data to detect hardware faults such as damaged propellers.

Assistive Vision & Safe Guidance

Wearable vision systems, built with Meta Project Aria glasses, that track and predict the motion of surrounding objects to guide drivers and visually impaired people along safe paths.

Research Experience

AirLab, Carnegie Mellon UniversityMay 2026 – Nov 2026
Pittsburgh, PA · Advisor: Prof. Sebastian Scherer

Built a people-detection pipeline for the DARPA Triage Challenge. Curated a casualty-detection dataset of 12,000 frames and 30,000 bounding boxes, benchmarked RF-DETR, YOLO and GroundingDINO, and raised F0.5 from 0.418 to 0.644. Also developed an early-fusion thermal–NIR pipeline that cuts false nighttime detections in a single inference pass.

Learning, Communications, and Wireless Networks Lab, UCFSep 2025 – present
Orlando, FL · Advisor: Prof. Nazanin Rahnavard

Developing a Project Aria–based guidance system for drivers and visually impaired people: a real-time 20 FPS pipeline on RGB and stereo data that predicts obstacle motion and uses model predictive control for collision-aware guidance. Mentoring a team of 6 undergraduate students.

Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems Lab, UCFSep 2025 – Dec 2025
Orlando, FL · Advisor: Prof. Truong Nghiem

Contributed to a loosely coupled SLAM framework fusing IMU, three RGB-D cameras and LiDAR for forests with changing light.

LHCb Experiment, CERNSep 2024 – Nov 2024
Geneva, Switzerland · Advisor: Dr. Benjamin Couturier

Built an interactive in-browser Python exercise (PyScript, Pyodide) for LHCb high-school outreach, then organized CERN lectures for about 200 Ukrainian students with Vilnius University.

Automatic Control Laboratory, Lakehead UniversityMay 2024 – Aug 2024
Thunder Bay, Canada · Advisor: Prof. Abdelhamid Tayebi

Worked on vision-based indoor navigation of quadrotors with QDrone and Crazyflie platforms, motion capture and sensor fusion.

Quadrotor UAV Study, Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic InstituteMay 2022 – Jun 2025
Kyiv, Ukraine · Advisor: Prof. Oleksandr Muraviov

Developed attitude estimation and inertial navigation for a mini drone, fusing IMU and optical flow with Extended Kalman and complementary filters. Designed experiments on vibration, acoustic and IMU data from damaged propellers to build a multimodal damage-detection system.
